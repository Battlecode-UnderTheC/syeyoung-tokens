# syeyoung-tokens

Strategies for the unswbc dragons game, written by agents and measured by a league.

- **Orchestrator** (a session asked to run generations): read `ORCHESTRATOR.md` in full and follow it.
- **Worker** (a subagent writing one variant): your brief is your instructions
  (template: `league/BRIEF.md`). Write only your own `variants/<name>/`, in your own worktree.
- Anyone else: `README.md` (layout, workflow) and `DESIGN.md` (the plan).

Never look in `../syeyoung-tokens.league/maps/holdout/`, never push or submit to the
competition server without the user, and never print `~/.unswbc/keys.json`.
