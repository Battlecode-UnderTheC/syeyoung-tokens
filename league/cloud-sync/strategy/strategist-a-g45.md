Change: before kHungerUntil (r150), a non-queen worker is idle when sched gave it no tile within kSchedHorizon or its best food goal needs waiting (ready > dist). An idle worker gets credit on every reachable tile not seen for S+ rounds: min(1, age/S) * explore_value * hunger_mult / (d+1). S = the median countdown this dragon has read on beds it saw (floor 10 until any). Contest and danger discounts unchanged; queen excluded; no roles. Label `stale: patrol`, counted.
