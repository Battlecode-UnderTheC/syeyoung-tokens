## What we learned

- Every team beat its round-1 bot. The common gain was a sonar-agreed champion fed late.
- free's biggest leak was portal starvation: it never crossed a portal with an unseen exit (schooltime seed 1: 6 dragons to v8's 20 at r50).
- search: letting other heads move in the survival test cut devil seed-1 deaths 476 to 157.
- swarm: the best consolidation time depends on team size and map (early feeding wins stronghold and autarky, loses big_empty).
- Pearl supply feeds back: a bed refills only after its pearl is eaten, so the side that covers beds sooner gets more pearls.
