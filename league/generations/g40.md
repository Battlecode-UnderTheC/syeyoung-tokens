## Next
- g41 candidate: sonar-shared sightings of enemy stubs, so long workers get earlier warning (g40-2 found killers come from beyond the 7x7 view).
The user's ideas for g41 (2026-10-05):
- **Pearl countdowns.** Use observed countdowns more fully, accounting for the enemy's side: the enemy may take a mirror pearl, and whether a pearl is still sitting there or a dragon lies on it changes what spawns when the pair comes due. Reason about contested beds from what's observed on both sides. (Engine: a bed and its mirror share one countdown; at due time each tile spawns only if empty and unoccupied, and one new gap is drawn for the pair. See shared/engine/dragons/game.cpp BeginRound.)
- **Mirrored countdowns for raids** (user: 'you can exploit symmetry'). Once symmetry is committed, an observed countdown gives the exact spawn round of the enemy-side mirror bed. Use it to time raids and contests and to skip, and maybe to deny by occupying the tile. This is a rules-based edge, not an opponent exploit.
- **Exploration.** Rush toward unseen ground rather than the board centre (g40-5 tests this).
