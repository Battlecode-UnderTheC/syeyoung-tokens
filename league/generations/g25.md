## What we learned

- **The measurement problem.** `sim` and the league do not reproduce the judge.
  - Every variant since v7 that beat it 55-60% in sim or the league came out 19-24 of 44 in the judge sandbox: gst100, 101, 115, 127, 128, 131.
  - Even at the engine's own seed, individual games differ between sim and the sandbox.
  - Until that is fixed, judge variants with `scripts/sbx-duel.sh` (multi-seed sandbox).
- Early deaths of short dragons come from crowding at spawns and splits (from r14), not from goal choice or dead ends.
- The opening nearest-pearl preference is live (removing it: 27-35). Simpler dead-end pricing wins in sim.
