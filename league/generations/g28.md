## What we learned

- hunter jumped 13 points with two changes: patrol goals that cover beds sooner (r100 dragons 17.9 to 21.0) and feeding from champion length 5.
- territory's free-space check counted unseen edges as exits, trapping small dragons in blind corridors. Counting unseen as 3 tiles cut schooltime trapped deaths 148 to 61 a match.
- swarm: spreading dragons further apart loses. The line is at about 53% vs v8 and tuning has run out.
- free: avoiding lunge reach lost trades on small maps, because the remains lie exactly there.
- Most losses to v8 are eliminations on small crowded maps (devil, trophy, arena, default_small, dilemma).
