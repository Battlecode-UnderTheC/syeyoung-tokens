## What we learned so far

- **Run-to-run variance.** gst-search-8 and gst-search-6 swapped 42-36 and 36-42 on the identical 78 games vs queen-3. Single-matchup splits of about 78 games are noise.
- **The guard costs the opening on cramped boards.** On stripes and weakhold the guarded queen barely eats or splits from r20 (6.9 vs 13.9 dragons at r100). On stripes the whole queen line loses, so it is the queen-as-champion logic, not only the guard.
- **Free-step runs pay late.** gst-search-8's champions died to enemy lunges 19 times in 80 sim games; escape runs cut that to 14.
- **The queen dies "stupidly".** 14 of 22 queen-guard-7 deaths were the queen trapped at length 2-3 with no enemy near, mostly after stepping into a 1-wide pocket herself. Sim fixes (qg9, qg10) didn't carry to the arena.
- **The late economy collapses after r320** vs codex-queen-value-1: heir feeding halves our material while codex grows a long queen.
- **The crossover works.** gst-search-7's economy with qg7's guard is the only clear win (64%) and wins queen-decided games 20-1.
- **gst-search-10's queen survives** (alive at r300 0.50, was 0.10) but it trails on board control and loses to queen-guard-7 24-54.
