## What we learned

- **The split window is the best tweak of the generation** (gst13, +80 Elo on gst5, best
  holdout so far at 77%); **small gains stack** (gst15 = three small ones, 27-13 vs gst12).
- **Split at 4 is load-bearing** even on worldgen: split at 6 lost 13-49 overall.
- **Mirroring the map, sprints, assassins, a leader split window: no gain.** Terrain
  knowledge doesn't prevent traps; allies' bodies do the trapping.
- **Turn order:** disputed (g4-3 vs g4-9); a real first-mover edge exists for swarm-hunt play
  on the official maps.
