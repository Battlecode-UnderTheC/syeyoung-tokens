## What we learned

- The adversarial reply is the mechanism that pays: search-6 beat its parent and v8 on held-out seeds.
- Consolidation mechanisms didn't pay in any line: pantry, den/parking, one-champion agreement, delivery. Feeder deaths are only 5-11 a match; the champion grows mostly by eating. The gap between lines is economy before r300.
- Late hunting of the enemy champion doesn't pay: v8's champion almost never enters our view.
- giant: moving the champion by fear of lunge reach instead of by lookahead cost about 13 wins in 90 vs v8.
- `check`'s slowest turn is a load artefact; measure CPU in the judge sandbox (search-5 on big_empty: max 45.9M of 100M points).
