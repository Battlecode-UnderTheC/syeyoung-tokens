## What we learned

- **Endgame consolidation is the big lever:** one sonar-agreed champion that workers feed
  took gather-split-trade-1 up ~70 Elo (29-11 head to head); the same port into
  counter-swarm-1 (counter-swarm-5, g3-1) rated 2017 vs 1987.
- **Defensive changes lost:** dodging lunges/strikes (gather-split-trade-3,
  counter-swarm-4) and the look-ahead safety layer on counter-swarm-1 (counter-swarm-2)
  were even or worse. Strikes/lunges cause only ~10–25 of 100+ head-to-head deaths a match.
- **Hunting the enemy's last dragons** (counter-swarm-3) rarely finds them; eliminations
  didn't drop.
- Worker sims of 60–100 matches did not predict league results for ±2–5-win changes.
