## What we learned

- A from-scratch bot beats v8 for the first time, by removing caution that cost economy.
- Noise floor: the same bot against itself over 150 games came out 66-6-78. Differences under about 12 wins in 150 are noise.
- Tuning is at its noise floor in every line; the next gain needs a new mechanism.
- Late big maps: both sides sit at the 64 cap; v8's total rises after r380 while a feed-by-dying team's total falls.
- The champion eats at most one pearl a round; stopping 2-3-long dragons from feeding collapses results (54-95, swarm).
