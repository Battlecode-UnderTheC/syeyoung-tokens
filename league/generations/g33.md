## What we learned

- Queens almost always die (median about r50 on the server). v12 had no champion after its queen died, so nothing was fed; the heir champion fixed that.
- A guard keeps the queen alive (21-30% vs 8%) and wins queen-decided games, but costs early economy (13 vs 19 dragons at r100); on the heir base with queen splits allowed, the cost goes away.
- On dilemma, autarky and slithery_fight both queens are doomed at spawn.
- Sim vs judge divergence was floating-point contraction (FMA), not static state. Fixed with -ffp-contract=off everywhere.
- Arena metrics: board control and log(dragons) predict r50-350, the longest dragon r400+; only-my-queen-alive at r300 wins 84%.
- unswbc 1.2.7: same rules, 8 official maps changed and the Sprint maps added.
