## What we learned

- **Striking at enemy heads with multi-step moves** is the biggest gain: all three new
  leaders do it (counter-swarm-1's strikes, swarm-hunt-1's lunge: turning it off cost ~14
  wins in 80).
- **Target sharing** (yield a pearl to a nearer visible ally) was gather-split-trade-1's
  largest gain and helped counter-swarm-1 and swarm-hunt-1 too.
- **strategy-v1 had a bug:** with no goal, `SafeStep` tried directions −1, 0, 1, 2 (never
  West). Fixing it alone went 30-30 → 38-21 in counter-swarm-1's sims. All three leaders fix it.
- **Trapped deaths come from our own allies,** not corridors; stricter room checks
  (gather-split-trade-2) made things slightly worse.
- **A lone giant loses; a fed giant is about even with strategy-v1.** Sonar gossip reaches
  few dragons (giant-1, sonar-pack-1); sharing pearl positions is useless when pathing only
  uses edges a dragon has seen itself.
- **Remaining losses are longest-dragon losses** with far more total length spread over ~15
  dragons (counter-swarm-1, gather-split-trade-1).
