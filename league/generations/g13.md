## What we learned

- Watching replays pays: the league-1 watch found the known-portal one-way door and the slow opening;
  both fixes (gst42, ls17) and their ports now hold #1 and #2.
- The economy is the live lever: charging bed waiting and valuing clusters won in all 5 lines it
  entered (12-22 games in ~100). Its constants are near best.
- Portal crossing matters more on unseen maps than in sims (gst42 even in sims, 67% holdout).
- Dead ends: sonar target claims, enemy-head warnings for safety, goal stickiness, the trader
  switch, regional/pocket champions, portal-pocket farming (pearls up, wins flat).
- "Cut off from the champion" mostly means unexplored ground, not portals.
- Process: check worker results that look copied (ls19 reported pre-fix numbers after a fix); port
  scoring code with its performance fixes (a literal port was 20-150× slower); gate accept on a tag.
