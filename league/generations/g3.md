## What we learned

- **Consolidation transfers across lineages** (cs5 +, swh4 +), and stacks with the lunge (gst5).
- **Feed timing is noise** within 300–360 (four tweaks, mixed); 260 is clearly worse.
- **Nothing about the champion's logistics or safety pays:** holding it, routing feeders,
  gathering en route, guarding it. The champion almost never dies; late games are lost on
  how much team is left at the feed round.
- **Eliminations are lost on numbers,** and gating trades by our count didn't reduce them.
- **Bed countdowns are fully periodic** (never pause), and symmetry can be read off them;
  but mirrored info alone was neutral because pathing can't reach unseen tiles.
- **Judge CPU:** ~4–6.5M points a turn for the leaders (limit 100M).
